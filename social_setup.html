UbiClipper account setup

Export All posts clips through each platform's official API. Every platform requires you to create your own (free) developer app once and paste its two keys into UbiClipper. This takes about 10–20 minutes per platform. Your keys and login tokens stay on your PC, encrypted with your Windows account.

The redirect URI UbiClipper uses for TikTok and Instagram is http://localhost:8765/callback. For YouTube it is http://127.0.0.1:8765/callback. Copy them exactly.

TikTok (sends clips to your drafts)

  1. Go to developers.tiktok.com and log in with your TikTok account. Click Manage apps → Connect an app and create an app (any name, e.g. "UbiClipper personal").
  2. At the top of the app page, switch to Sandbox and create a sandbox. A sandbox works immediately without TikTok reviewing your app.
  3. Under Products, add Login Kit and Content Posting API.
  4. In Login Kit, choose platform Desktop and add the redirect URI http://localhost:8765/callback.
  5. Under Scopes, make sure user.info.basic and video.upload are added.
  6. In the sandbox settings, under Target users, add your own TikTok account.
  7. Copy the Client key and Client secret from the app page.
  8. In UbiClipper: Accounts → TikTok → Connect…, paste both, click Log in & connect and approve in the browser window that opens.

Clips arrive in your TikTok inbox / drafts: you get a notification in the TikTok app, then add music or effects and press Post yourself. Posting straight to your profile requires TikTok to audit the app, which they don't do for personal tools.

Instagram Reels

Before you start

  1. Your Instagram account must be a Creator or Business account (Instagram → Settings → Account type and tools → Switch to professional account).
  2. It must be linked to a Facebook Page (create one if needed, then in the Page's settings → Linked accounts → Instagram).

Create the app

  1. Go to developers.facebook.com/apps and click Create app. Choose the Business type (or the use case "Manage everything on your Page / Instagram").
  2. Add the product Facebook Login for Business. In its Settings, add http://localhost:8765/callback under Valid OAuth Redirect URIs and save.
  3. Make sure these permissions are available to the app (App Review → Permissions and features; standard access is enough while you are the app's admin): instagram_basic, instagram_content_publish, pages_show_list, business_management.
  4. Open App settings → Basic and copy the App ID and App secret.
  5. In UbiClipper: Accounts → Instagram → Connect…, paste both and log in with the Facebook account that manages the Page. Tick your Page and Instagram account when asked.

The app can stay in development mode: as the admin you can post to your own account. The login lasts about 60 days; after that, press Reconnect….

YouTube Shorts

  1. Go to console.cloud.google.com and create a new project (e.g. "UbiClipper").
  2. Open APIs & Services → Library, search YouTube Data API v3 and click Enable.
  3. Open OAuth consent screen (Google Auth Platform): user type External, fill in the app name and your email. Leave it in Testing and add your own Google account under Test users.
  4. Open Credentials → Create credentials → OAuth client ID, application type Desktop app. Copy the Client ID and Client secret.
  5. In UbiClipper: Accounts → YouTube → Connect…, paste both and log in. Google warns that the app isn't verified: click Continue (it's your own app).

Two Google rules for unverified personal apps: uploads are locked to private until the project passes Google's API audit (you can switch each video to public in YouTube Studio), and while the consent screen is in Testing the login expires after 7 days, so press Reconnect… weekly. Vertical clips of 60 s or less are shown as Shorts automatically.

Troubleshooting

  1. "redirect_uri mismatch": the URI in the developer app must match exactly, including http, the port 8765 and /callback.
  2. Browser says "can't connect" after logging in: UbiClipper must stay open while you log in, and nothing else may use port 8765.
  3. Instagram: "no Instagram business account found": the Instagram account isn't a professional account or isn't linked to the Page you selected during login.
  4. Something expired: open Accounts and press Reconnect…. Your keys are remembered.
  5. Already-posted clips are marked in the Export All window so you don't post twice.
